Prompt1: Create a concept sketch in clean vector graphics that uses a sea metaphor as the overall structural vehicle to encode the data of urban population growth over the past decade. Data Tenor: rising growth (continuous growth trend). Visual mapping (one-to-one, no numerical renderings): Population maps to the size of wave crests (wave crest size), Time maps to the horizontal position along a tide line (tide line position). Composition and read path: arrange a sequence of wave crests along a gently curving tide line, left to right to reflect chronological progression; crest heights increase from left to right to express the rising urban population; the baseline tide line acts as the spatial conduit for time; ensure the reading path follows left-to-right along the tide line. Visual style and constraints: minimal, abstract, and sketch-like; foreground/midground/background with clear depth separation; pure white background; vector lines with crisp edges; no shading. Color palette: restrained cool blues and black lines; no textures or photographic realism. No axes, no ticks, no gridlines (allow only extremely faint alignment cues if necessary, not forming a grid). Annotations embedded in the metaphor as data mapping notes: "Population → wave crest size; Time → tide line position" written subtly within the scene or as a tiny, unobtrusive caption near the tide line. Spatial details: three-layer composition (foreground waves and crests, midground tide line, background white space) to preserve whitespace and readability. Theme note: emphasize metaphor as structure and trend expression rather than precise values; the design should feel like a deliberate, 30% complete conceptual sketch, leaving 70% for designer extension, while clearly communicating the growing trend through the sea-wave metaphor.
